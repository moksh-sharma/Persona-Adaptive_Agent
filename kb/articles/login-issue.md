# Login Issue

If login fails: confirm email and password, reset password via the Forgot Password link, clear browser cache/cookies for our domain, disable VPN or ad-blockers temporarily, and verify your account has not been locked after repeated failures. If SSO is enabled, validate IdP SAML/OIDC settings with your administrator.
